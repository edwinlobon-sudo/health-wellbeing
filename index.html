🎯 Course Overview & Learning Objectives

Welcome to the Health and Well-Being module! This course is designed to develop students' English communicative competence through meaningful contexts related to daily habits, physical wellness, stress management, and emotional support.

Lesson 1 Objectives: Physical Health & Daily Routines

  • Identify key vocabulary related to physical health, nutrition, and workout routines[cite: 10].
  • Recognize how daily physical choices impact long-term health and well-being[cite: 10].
  • Describe personal daily routines using target action verbs in written and oral format[cite: 10].

Lesson 2 Objectives: Mental Health, Stress & Expressing Advice

  • Learn and correctly apply modal verbs for giving advice (should, ought to, could, would)[cite: 9].
  • Understand stress management vocabulary and conduct personal stress self-assessments[cite: 9].
  • Provide constructive and polite recommendations to peers experiencing stress or anxiety[cite: 9].

🇨🇴 PRUEBA ICFES SABER 11 - LESSON 1: PHYSICAL HEALTH

Responde las 5 preguntas de opción múltiple con única respuesta basadas en lectura comprensiva y gramática en contexto.

READING TEXT:
"Sarah wakes up at 6:00 AM every single day. Before having breakfast, she drinks two glasses of water and goes for a 30-minute jog in the park. She believes that small morning routines transform overall health. Later, she prepares a meal rich in proteins and fiber, avoiding processed sugar. Sarah rarely feels tired during her workday."
1. What is Sarah's immediate action after waking up?
2. According to the text, what does Sarah restrict in her daily diet?
3. Complete the sentence: "If you want to build a healthy lifestyle, you _____ practice physical activity consistently."
4. Which of the following is categorized as an 'Unhealthy Habit'?
5. What is the synonym of 'jogging' in the context of physical activity?

🇨🇴 PRUEBA ICFES SABER 11 - LESSON 2: MENTAL HEALTH & ADVICE

Responde las 5 preguntas contextualizadas sobre manejo del estrés, ansiedad y uso correcto de verbos modales.

READING TEXT:
"Academic pressure often triggers anxiety in students. Psychologist Dr. Miller suggests that when individuals feel overwhelmed, they shouldn't isolate themselves. Instead, they could talk to a trusted friend or counselor. Furthermore, practicing deep breathing exercises for five minutes can instantly calm the nervous system."
1. What is Dr. Miller's advice when a student feels overwhelmed?
2. Student A: "I have three exams tomorrow and I can't concentrate!"
Student B: "If I were you, I _____ take a short walk to clear my mind."
3. Which sentence correctly uses a modal verb to express polite advice?
4. According to the reading, what quick activity can calm the nervous system?
5. What is the main purpose of using modal verbs like 'should' and 'could' in daily conversation?
